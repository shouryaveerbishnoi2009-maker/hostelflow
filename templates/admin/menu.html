🧠 How Dish Prep Influences Crowd Flow

HostelFlow uses dish-aware modeling. When an item with higher preparation time is on the menu, student wait times increase linearly.

🟢 Fast Serving (1–6 mins)

🟡 Moderate (7–12 mins)

Items requiring frequent griddle replenishments like Puri Sabzi, Rajma Chawal, Chole Bhature.

🔴 Made-to-Order (13+ mins)

Items cooked per student like Stuffed Parathas, Fresh Dosas, Dum Biryani. Highest queue bottleneck.
